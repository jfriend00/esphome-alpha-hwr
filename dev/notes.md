FYI, a slightly related comment on sensor UI.  This may just be a result of how HA works and perhaps there isn't a better way to present it in HA (or it's more work than it justifies), but when you first bring up this component, you have setpoints for every mode, regardless of whether you ever use them or not - constant flow setpoint, constant pressure setpoint, constant speed setpoint, proportional pressure setpoint, two cycle time settings (for cycle time control), two temperature settings (for temperature control mode).
